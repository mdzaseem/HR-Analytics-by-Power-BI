# HR Analytics Dashboard using Power BI

## About the Project

I created this dashboard using Power BI to understand employee attrition and explore different workforce trends.
I wanted to see how attrition varies across age groups, education fields, salary levels, and job roles.

The dashboard brings important HR metrics together in one place and includes department filters to explore the
data from different perspectives.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Filters and Charts

## What I Analyzed

- Total employees and employee attrition
- Attrition rate by age group and gender
- Attrition by education field and job role
- Attrition across salary slabs
- Employee attrition by years at the company
- Department-wise HR analysis

## Key Findings

- The dashboard shows 1,470 employees and 237 attrition cases, with an attrition rate of 16.1%.
- The 26–35 age group has the highest attrition count, with 116 cases.
- Laboratory Technicians have the highest attrition count by job role, with 62 cases.
- The salary slab up to 5K accounts for 163 attrition cases.
- Life Sciences represents the largest share of attrition by education field, at 38%.

## What I Learned

This project helped me practice creating interactive Power BI visuals and using HR data to identify patterns in employee attrition.
It also helped me understand how dashboards can make workforce data easier to explore and interpret.
